How this target is calculated

NutriTrack estimates maintenance calories from your profile, then works out the average deficit needed to move from your current weight to your target weight by the selected date. The planner limits the deficit to about 1,000 kcal/day and keeps a calorie floor. Weight loss is not perfectly linear, so treat the result as a starting target and adjust using your real trend over several weeks.
